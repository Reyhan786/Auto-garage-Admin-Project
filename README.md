# 🚗 Auto Garage Management System (Salesforce)

A complete **Auto Garage / Service Center Management System** built on **Salesforce Lightning Platform**.  
This project helps manage **customers, vehicles, repair jobs, invoices, payments, reports, and dashboards** in one place.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This system is designed for **small to medium auto garages** where:
- Many customers are **individual (no contact needed)**
- Each vehicle has **repair/service jobs**
- Each service job generates an **invoice (Pay Bill & Memo)**
- Business owners want **daily/monthly income & due payment tracking**

---

## 🧱 Objects Used

### 🔹 Standard Objects
- **Account** → Customer (mandatory)
- **Case** → Repair / Service Job
- **Report & Dashboard**

### 🔹 Custom Objects
- **Vehicle__c** → Customer vehicles
- **Invoice__c (Pay Bills & Memos)** → Billing & payment tracking
